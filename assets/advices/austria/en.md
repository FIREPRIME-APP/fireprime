# Preparation and Safety Advice in Case of Fire 
In case of fire, always follow the instructions of the authorities and stay informed through: 
- Civil Protection alerts on your mobile (112 outbound alert) 
- Firefighters and security forces on site 
- Official social media: @zivilschutz.at, @feuerwehr.at 
- Media (TV, radio) 


## Prepare your home from fire 
- A 2-meter strip without vegetation around the façade 
- 3 meters between trees so they do not touch  
- Tree pruning in the height of 3m so that there is no vertical connectivity of the vegetation
- Roofs, porches, and awnings clear of dry leaves 
- Prefer double glazing and protection with non-combustible shutters 
- No firewood or flammable materials in contact with the house (especially combustible balconies and roofs) 
- Fuels stored in ventilated and protected spaces 
- Chimneys and vents covered with metal mesh 
- Avoid fences and porches made of heather or thuja 
- Long, flexible garden hose ready to use 
- Emergency kit prepared, including first aid kit, flashlight, and radio 
- Don't store combustible or hazardous materials in the house 

## Prepare yourself 
- Inform yourself: is there an evacuation plan for your area? 
- Prepare an evacuation kit (first aid, personal hygiene products, flashlight etc.)

## What to do if you see fire
- Call the Fire Department IMMEDIATELY (tel.122 or 112) and provide clear information about your location, the direction of the fire and the type of vegetation that is being burnt

## What to do if fire approaches
The arrival of the fire front is very stressful and can last several minutes. Do not go outside, even if your instinct tells you to. Only evacuate if instructed by the authorities. 
- Stay connected (cellphone, internet, radio) and evacuate if you are told so 
- Inform the neighbors

**If you shelter in place:** 
- Close all doors (interior and exterior), windows, and lower shutters. 
- If you do not have time or instructions to evacuate, stay inside the house with your family and pets, in an interior room. 
- Disconnect gas, butane, or diesel supplies. Switch off electrical devices. (and if it is safe to do, move cylinders in a safe place) 
- Remove curtains if you have time. Move garden furniture and other combustible objects away from the façade. 
- Leave the car in the garage with the keys inside.  
- Wet exterior areas (without taking risks). 
- Keep access routes clear for emergency teams. 
- Seal smoke entry points (like door gaps) with wet cloths. 
- Protect yourself with cotton clothing. 
- Do not leave until it is safe to do so. Follow the authorities' instructions.

## In case of evacuation ordered by the authorities
- Remain calm 
- Lock the house and disconnect electricity and gas. 
- Head to the place indicated by security services. 
- Wear cotton clothing and proper footwear. 
- Take only essentials: identification, mobile phone, charger, money, and essential medication. 
- Assist children elderly and family members with reduced mobility
- Take your pets with you

## If the fire has reached your building:
- Stay indoors if you cannot leave securely 
- Do not enter the car, you are safer in a building made of non-combustible materials 
- Seal smoke entry points (like door gaps) with wet cloths. 
- Close the interior doors to slow down the spread of the fire. 
- Fill the bathtub, sinks, and buckets with backup water. 
- Gather everyone together in one room.  
- Breathe close to the floor with a wet cloth 
- Cover your head and the rest of the body and use a damp cloth to protect your face  
- Make sure you have a flashlight and spare batteries with you in case of a power outage.

## After the fire
- Leave the house and immediately extinguish any remaining small fires. 
- For at least 48 hours, check the perimeter and the outdoor areas of the house at regular intervals for possible flare-ups.
